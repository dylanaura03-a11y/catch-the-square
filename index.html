Welcome to Catch The Square!

Catch the moving square as quickly as you can, collect coins, build combos and try to set a new personal high score.

What is Catch The Square?

Catch The Square is a quick arcade reaction game designed around one simple challenge: find the square and catch it before the round ends. The game starts immediately when you press Start Game, so there is no complicated setup.

Each round gives you a limited amount of time and a limited number of lives. The square changes position during the round, requiring you to keep your eyes on the game area and react quickly.

Why play?

⚡ Reaction

Practice quick visual reactions by tracking the square as it appears in different locations.

🎯 Accuracy

Try to catch the target consistently and keep your combo going.

🪙 Collect Coins

Successful catches reward coins that can be used in the game's shop.

🏆 Beat Your Record

Your highest score is saved through the game's supported storage system so you can keep trying to improve your personal record.

Quick Start Guide

  4. Keep catching targets to increase your score and combo.
  5. Watch your lives and remaining time.
  6. Use coins in the Shop when you have enough.
  7. Try another round and beat your high score.

📖 How To Play

1. Start a Round

Press Start Game from the main menu. A new round begins with a 60-second timer and three lives.

2. Find The Square

Look inside the game area for the green square. Its position changes during gameplay.

3. Catch It

Click or tap the square when you see it. Successful catches increase your score, coins and combo.

4. Watch Your Stats

The top of the game screen displays your remaining time, lives, score, level, combo and coins.

5. Look For Bonuses

A golden bonus target can appear during the round. Catching it provides additional rewards.

6. Keep Improving

When your round ends, check your score and try again. The goal is to build a better personal high score over time.

🎯 Scoring Guide

Understanding the scoring system can help you plan your next high-score attempt.

🟩 Normal Catch

Every successful normal catch gives:

  • +1 Score
  • +1 Coin
  • +1 Combo

🔥 Combo Reward

Every fifth combo adds an additional coin reward.

This means consistent catching can increase your coin total faster.

⭐ Golden Bonus

Catching the golden bonus gives:

  • +5 Score
  • +10 Coins
  • +2 Combo

📈 Levels

Your level increases as your score grows. The game calculates level progression from your current score.
