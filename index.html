📖 How To Play

1. Start

Press Start Game to begin a 60-second round with three lives.

2. Catch

Click or tap the green square before it moves.

3. Build Combos

Successful catches increase your combo. Every five catches gives bonus coins.

4. Avoid Misses

Clicking empty space costs one life and resets your combo.

5. Beat Your Score

Keep playing to improve your high score and earn more coins.

ℹ️ About Catch The Square

What Is It?

Catch The Square is an independent browser reaction game built around quick rounds, changing target colors, level rewards, unlockable shapes, difficulty modes, combos, coins and unlockable skins.

Why Was It Made?

The goal is to provide a simple game that can be opened directly in a web browser without a complicated setup.

Your Progress

Game progress is saved locally in your browser. Clearing browser storage can remove saved progress.
