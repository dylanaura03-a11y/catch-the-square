🎮 Welcome To Catch The Square

Catch The Square is a simple arcade reaction game built around one main challenge: find the target and catch it before the round comes to an end.

Each round gives you 60 seconds and a life system. The target moves around the game area, so you need to keep watching and react quickly.

⚡ How The Game Works

👀 Watch

Keep your attention on the game area and look for the green square.

🖱️ Catch

Click or tap the square whenever you see it.

🪙 Earn

Successful catches reward coins that can be used in the shop.

🏆 Improve

Play more rounds and try to beat your personal high score.

💡 Tips For Players

  • Keep your eyes on the game area.
  • React quickly when the square appears.
  • Try to maintain a steady rhythm.
  • Watch your lives and timer.
  • Use bonus targets when they appear.
  • Replay rounds to improve your score.

📖 How To Play

1️⃣ Start

Press Start Game to begin a new 60-second round.

2️⃣ Find The Square

Look inside the game area for the green square.

3️⃣ Catch It

Click or tap the square to score points and earn coins.

4️⃣ Build Your Combo

Successful catches increase your combo.

5️⃣ Catch Bonuses

Golden bonus targets appear during gameplay and give additional rewards.

6️⃣ Beat Your Record

When the round finishes, try again and aim for a higher personal score.

🎯 Scoring Guide

🟩 Normal Catch

Each normal catch gives:

  • +1 score
  • +1 coin
  • +1 combo

🔥 Combo Reward

Every fifth combo gives an additional 5 coins.

⭐ Golden Bonus

A successful golden bonus gives:

  • +5 score
  • +10 coins
  • +2 combo

📈 Levels

Your level increases as your score increases.
