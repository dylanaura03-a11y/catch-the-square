📖 How To Play

1. Start

Press Start Game from the menu to begin a 60-second round.

2. Catch The Square

Click or tap the green square before it moves somewhere else.

3. Build Your Combo

Every successful catch increases your combo. Every 5 catches in a row gives bonus coins.

4. Watch Your Lives

Missing the square costs a life. Lose all three lives and the round ends.

5. Spend Coins

Use coins in the Shop to unlock different square skins.

ℹ️ About Catch The Square

What Is It?

Catch The Square is a simple reaction game where you try to catch a moving square as many times as possible before the round ends.

Why Play?

It is designed to be quick to understand and easy to play on both computers and phones.

Your Progress

Your coins, high score, games played, catches and unlocked skins are saved in your browser.

❓ FAQ

How long is a game?

Each round lasts up to 60 seconds unless you lose all your lives first.

Does the game save my progress?

Yes. The game uses your browser's local storage to save progress on the device.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. The game supports touch controls as well as mouse clicking.

What can I buy?

You can spend coins on Rainbow, Fire, Ice, Gold and Galaxy square skins.

Why isn't an ad showing?

Advertisements are controlled by the advertising system and may not appear every time the page loads.
