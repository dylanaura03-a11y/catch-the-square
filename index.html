📖 HOW TO PLAY

1. Start the game

Press START GAME to begin a 60-second round.

2. Catch the square

Click the green square whenever it appears in a new position. Each successful catch gives you points and coins.

3. Watch your lives

The square moves automatically. Missing it costs a life, so stay focused and react quickly.

4. Use bonus squares

Occasionally a golden bonus button appears. Click it to earn extra points and coins.

5. Build your score

Your level increases as your score rises. Try to beat your personal high score every time you play.

6. Spend your coins

Visit the Shop to spend coins on different square styles.

ℹ️ ABOUT CATCH THE SQUARE

Catch The Square is a simple arcade game built around speed, reaction time and high scores.

The goal is straightforward: catch the moving square as many times as possible before your time and lives run out.

Players can earn coins while playing, unlock items in the shop, complete achievements and submit scores to the global leaderboard.

The game is designed to be quick to understand and easy to play, while giving players a reason to keep practicing and improving.

🎮 Game Goal

Improve your reaction speed, build combos and see how high you can push your score.

❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Is Catch The Square free?

Yes. The game can be played directly from the website.

How do I earn coins?

You earn coins by successfully catching squares and bonus targets.

How do I get a high score?

Keep catching squares without missing them and use bonus targets when they appear.

Where can I see my high score?

Your personal high score is shown on the main menu and during gameplay.

How does the leaderboard work?

After playing, you can save your high score with a player name. The leaderboard displays the highest submitted scores.

What are the shop items?

Shop items are cosmetic square styles that can be purchased using coins earned from gameplay.
