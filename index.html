❓ How To Play

1. Start

Press START GAME to begin a 60-second round.


2. Catch

Click or tap the square whenever you see it.


3. Score

Each successful catch gives you one point and one coin.


4. Combo

Keep catching the square to build your combo. Every five catches gives you extra coins.


5. Bonus

Catch the golden bonus for extra points and coins.


6. Lives

If you miss the square before it moves, you lose one life.

ℹ️ About Catch The Square

Catch The Square is a browser reaction game built around a simple challenge: catch the moving square before time runs out.


The game is designed for quick rounds and repeat play. Players can improve their reaction skills, build combos, collect coins and chase higher scores.


Features

  • 60-second rounds
  • Three lives
  • Moving square
  • Combo bonuses
  • Golden bonus target
  • Coins
  • Unlockable skins
  • Missions
  • High-score tracking
  • Mobile support

💡 FAQ

How long does a game last?

A round lasts up to 60 seconds.


How do I earn coins?

Catching the square gives you coins. Combo bonuses and golden bonuses can give you additional coins.


Are my coins saved?

Yes. Your game progress is stored in your browser.


Can I play on a phone?

Yes. The game works with touch controls and adapts to smaller screens.


What are the skins?

Skins change the visual appearance of the square. You can unlock them using coins.
