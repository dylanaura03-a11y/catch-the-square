Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Welcome to Catch The Square. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our website and play the game.

Information Stored on Your Device

Catch The Square uses your browser's local storage to save game-related information such as your high score, coins, games played, catches, achievements, and selected cosmetic items.

This information is stored on your device and is used to provide game features and remember your progress.

Google Analytics

Catch The Square uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage, such as visits and how people interact with the website. Google Analytics may collect information such as browser information, device information, approximate location, and website interactions.

Google handles this information according to its own privacy policies.

Advertising

Catch The Square may display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements.

Advertising practices may change over time as the website and its advertising services are updated.

Cookies

Our website and third-party services such as Google Analytics and Google AdSense may use cookies or similar technologies.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Third-Party Services

Catch The Square may use third-party services including Google Analytics and Google AdSense. These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Children's Privacy

Catch The Square is a general-audience online game. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the game.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, game, or services change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
