About Catch The Square

What is Catch The Square?

Catch The Square is a free browser game where you try to catch a moving square before time runs out.

The game is designed to be simple to start, easy to understand, and fun to replay while trying to improve your score.

How It Works

Each game gives you a limited amount of time and a number of lives. Catch the moving square to increase your score and build combos.

You can also earn coins and unlock different cosmetic skins.

Features

  • 60-second gameplay rounds
  • Score and high-score tracking
  • Combo system
  • Coins and unlockable skins
  • Missions and achievements
  • Works directly in a web browser

Our Goal

The goal of Catch The Square is to provide a quick and enjoyable browser gaming experience without requiring a download.
